The Italian Renaissance (14th–17th century) was a transformative cultural movement that began in Florence and spread across Europe. Key figures include Leonardo da Vinci — the archetypal Renaissance man, excelling in art, science, and engineering — and Michelangelo, who created iconic works like the Sistine Chapel ceiling and the statue of David. Raphael perfected the ideals of Renaissance art through harmonious compositions and naturalistic figures. On the intellectual side, Petrarch is often called the "Father of Humanism," laying the philosophical groundwork for the entire movement.

Image 3 — Feature Highlights

PDF AI Agent

Upload any PDF and chat with it — powered by RAG, LangChain & OpenAI

📄
PDF Upload & Indexing
Upload any PDF and watch it get chunked, embedded, and indexed in real-time with WebSocket progress updates.
🔍
HyDE Retrieval
Hypothetical Document Embeddings generate a hypothetical answer first, then search — dramatically improving retrieval accuracy.
💬
Conversational Memory
Multi-turn context lets you ask follow-up questions naturally. "How big is it?" after "Tell me about Mars" just works.
📌
Cited Sources
Every answer shows which PDF pages were used, with expandable excerpts and direct PDF download links.
🔐
Per-User Isolation
Each user's documents live in their own Qdrant collection — no cross-user data leakage.
⚡
Async Job Queue
BullMQ + Redis processes PDFs in the background so the UI never blocks. Socket.io pushes "Ready!" the moment indexing completes.
🗂
PDF Library
Upload multiple PDFs, select which ones to query, and manage your document library with delete support.
📝
Chat History
Conversations persist across sessions — browse, resume, or delete past chats from the sidebar, like ChatGPT.
🏗
Production Architecture
Next.js · Express · LangChain · OpenAI · Qdrant · PostgreSQL · BullMQ · Socket.io · Clerk — all containerised with Docker.
